About me
======

I am a psychologist by training. I hold a B. Sc. in Psychology and a M. Sc. (Merit) in Neuroimaging for Clinical and Cognitive Neuroscience. I obtained PhD (Dr. rer. nat) with _Summa cum laude_ in November 2024.

My PhD research focused on structural and functional changes across cortical depth and layers during human somatosensory aging, employing Ultra-High Field 7 Tesla MRI as the primary approach and comparing the findings with mouse models using Two-Photon Calcium imaging and histological analysis.

Currently I work as a postdoctoral researcher at AG Tranlsational Imaging of Cortical Microstructure (Kuehn Lab), Hertie Institute for Clinical Brain Research (HIH), Tübingen, Germany. My postdoc research focus is on the tactile memory storage & retrieval mechanisms, tactile memory modulation pathways, and the brain plasticity change after self-intervention on somatic symptoms.

I also hold a Hertie Network of Excellence in Clinical Neuroscience fellowship at the moment, which allows me to extend my research towards age-related changes in body memory.
