It was very exciting and inspiring for me to attend Hertie Network of Excellence in Clinical Neuroscience Kick-off Symposium in Berlin!
It was an honour to be one of the twenty-four early-career researchers from Berlin, Bonn, Hamburg, Heidelberg/Mannheim, Munich, and Tübingen joining this exceptional program.
The kick-off symposium impressively demonstrated what the Hertie Network stands for: bringing excellent basic and clinical research closer together, providing Fellows with the space to pursue rigorous scientific work, and simultaneously fostering their development as future leaders. This process is supported by a scientific professional development program within the Hertie Academy of Clinical Neuroscience.
The next symposium will be held in November at our Tübingen site! Cannot wait to see all the fellows again!

[More information here](https://www.ghst.de/hertie-network)
